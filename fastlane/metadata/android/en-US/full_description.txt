<p><strong>Residex</strong> helps candidates follow medical residency selection calendars in one place.</p>
<h2>Key features</h2>
<ul>
<li>Search and filter selections by state and current stage</li>
<li>See registrations and exams scheduled within the next seven days</li>
<li>Follow the selections that matter to you and arrange them manually</li>
<li>View deadlines, fees, exam dates and official links</li>
<li>Receive configurable local notifications and send a test notification</li>
<li>Keep a local cache available between synchronizations</li>
</ul>
<h2>Data synchronization</h2>
<p>Residex synchronizes selection data with the service configured by the project. Authorized administrators can sign in to add, update or remove records.</p>
<h2>Privacy</h2>
<p>The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries. Notification and selection preferences are stored locally. Administrative credentials are sent only when the user explicitly signs in.</p>
